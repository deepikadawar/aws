PROJECT / TRANSPARENCY

Methodology & limitations

A transparent starting point for combining heat exposure and water-access indicators into a planning workflow.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Explain the score. Keep humans in control.

HeatWater helps people investigate potential needs, not make autonomous emergency decisions. Recommendations should be traceable to visible indicators and checked against ground truth.

01 / COMPOSITE SCORE

Vulnerability index

Each indicator is scaled from 0–100 in the demo. Higher scores indicate greater concern for heat, exposure or service gaps.

V = 0.28H + 0.25(100 − W) + 0.22E + 0.13(100 − G) + 0.12(100 − S)
  • H = heat indicator
  • W = water access
  • E = community exposure
  • G = green cover
  • S = service access

Weights are illustrative choices, not scientifically validated coefficients.

02 / RESOURCE RANKING

Priority heuristic

The planner ranks zones using a demonstrative blend of vulnerability, normalized population, and water-access gap.

P = 0.55V + 0.20(Pop / 100) + 0.25(100 − W)

This is not a facility-location optimization algorithm. It does not model travel distance, road access, demand uncertainty or station feasibility.

03 / PROPOSED DATA PIPELINE

How a production version could work

01

Collect

Verified weather, land-surface temperature, water-point and demographic datasets.

02

Standardize

Align spatial units, time periods, scales, missing values and provenance.

03

Validate

Check results against field reports and community/official feedback.

What this demo does not claim

  • No live weather or water-point data.
  • Zones and indicator values are simulated.
  • Grid is not geographically accurate.
  • Population figures are placeholders.
  • Scores are not official warnings or health-risk predictions.

What is needed next

  • Use verified, licensed datasets with timestamps.
  • Document sources, resolution and data gaps.
  • Consult public-health and water-service partners.
  • Test score sensitivity and fairness.
  • Validate proposed sites on the ground.
